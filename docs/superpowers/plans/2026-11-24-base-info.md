# Base Info Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** In the story base, clicking a soldier's name opens a stat card, and every building panel has an INFO button that opens a plain explanation of the building, both built from the game's live numbers.

**Architecture:** Two pure functions in `src/core/world` produce the data (`soldierStats`, `buildingInfo`). A new screen module draws a modal card and a modal info box over the existing panel or squad screen. Hit testing gains a `name` area on soldier rows (barracks, training, squad) and an `info` button on every panel; `App` holds two small modal states that any click, BACK or Escape closes.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, Canvas 2D (menu space 480x400), Vitest. Run: `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-laser-tribute-base-info-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- No rule, cost or number changes; Tutorial, Campaign and the mission soldier card are untouched. Opening or closing a card or box changes no world state and writes no save.
- Text uses the 5x7 uppercase font: only supported characters (`unsupportedChars(text) === []`), at most 8 wrapped rows per info box, nothing outside 0..480.
- No numbers typed into explanation text: they come from `BASE_RULES`, `levelsOf`, `incomePerDay`, `RANKS`, `CONFIG` and the world.
- The name area of a soldier row is the first `NAME_W = 120` px of the row (the spec's "about 210" was narrowed so most of the row keeps its old job); a click there never selects or picks, and a click elsewhere on the row behaves exactly as today.
- No gendered pronouns in text ("the soldier", never "he/she").

## Review Focus

- The card's HP, AP and accuracy equal a mission soldier's of the same rank, including a soldier whose training points push them over a rank threshold (Task 1).
- The name area opens the card without selecting or picking, the rest of the row still selects (barracks, training) and toggles the pick (squad), and a training or wounded soldier (not pickable in the squad step) still gets a card from the name area (Tasks 2, 3).
- Any click, BACK or Escape closes the card or box; leaving the screen closes them; no world or save change (Task 3).
- Each explanation shows live numbers at several research levels and wraps to at most 8 rows inside the box (Tasks 1, 2).
- Card and box draw inside the screen over a full 12-soldier barracks with supported characters (Task 2).

### Task 1: The data: soldier stats and building explanations

**Files:**
- Create: `src/core/world/soldierinfo.ts`, `src/core/world/buildinginfo.ts`
- Test: `tests/soldierinfo.test.ts`, `tests/buildinginfo.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `PoolSoldier`, `World`, `incomePerDay` from `./world`; `RANKS`, `rankFor` from `../ranks`; `CONFIG` from `../config`; `BASE_RULES`, `levelsOf`, `bedCount` from `./base`.
- Produces:
  ```ts
  // soldierinfo.ts
  export interface SoldierStats {
    name: string; rank: string; hp: number; ap: number; accuracy: number;   // accuracy in percent (the rank bonus)
    rows: [label: string, value: string][]; note: string;
  }
  export function soldierStats(s: PoolSoldier, w: World): SoldierStats;
  // buildinginfo.ts
  export type InfoId = 'lab' | 'market' | 'barracks' | 'training' | 'hospital' | 'wait';
  export interface BuildingInfo { title: string; lines: string[] }
  export function buildingInfo(id: InfoId, w: World): BuildingInfo;
  ```

`soldierStats`: `points = s.kills + s.trained`; `rank = rankFor(points)`; `hp = CONFIG.soldierHp + rank.hp`; `ap = CONFIG.maxAp + rank.ap`; `accuracy = Math.round(rank.accuracy * 100)`. `rows` in this order and with these labels and values: `RANK` the rank name uppercased; `HP`, `AP`, `ACCURACY` (`+8%` style); `KILLS`; `TRAINED`; `NEXT RANK` (`PRIVATE IN 2`, using the next rank's `minKills - points`, or `TOP RANK`); `STATUS` (`READY`, `TRAINING 2D`, `WOUNDED 3D`); and, only when the training level is at least 1, `TRAIN CAP` (`BASE_RULES.trainCaps[level - 1]` as a number of points, or `AT CAP` when `points` has reached it). `note` is `KIT IS CHOSEN BEFORE EACH FIGHT`.

`buildingInfo` drafts (the wording Rui reads; `{...}` are live values; `{pct}` rounds to a whole percent):
- **lab** (title `LAB`): "Research unlocks new gear and base upgrades." / "One project runs at a time and its credits are spent when it starts." / "Labs level {labs}: projects take {labFactor pct}% of their normal days." / "Better labs are a research project too."
- **barracks** (`BARRACKS`): "Your soldiers sleep here: {soldiers} of {beds} beds are in use." / "Hiring costs {hireCost} credits and needs a free bed." / "A fight needs four. Each missing soldier is replaced by a loaned mercenary for {mercFee} credits." / "More beds come from the Barracks projects in the lab."
- **market** (`MARKET`): "Gear you have not researched can still be bought on the equipment screen." / "It costs {premium pct}% of its normal price until the lab researches it." / "Researching an item removes the extra cost for good."
- **training** (`TRAINING`): level 0: "Not built yet: research Training facility I at the lab." Otherwise: "Training makes a ready soldier better without a fight." / "It takes {trainDays} days, one soldier at a time, and the soldier is out of the squad meanwhile." / "Each training adds a rank point. Level {level} trains up to {cap} points."
- **hospital** (`FIELD HOSPITAL`): "After a won fight, a soldier who fell may be saved instead of lost." / "Your second chance is {chance pct}% per fallen soldier (level {level} of 2)." / "A saved soldier is wounded for {woundedDays} days and cannot fight."
- **wait** (`WAIT`): "Waiting passes {waitOptions joined with ' OR '} days with nothing else done." / "Income arrives every day: now {incomePerDay} credits a day." / "Research, training and healing move forward." / "On harder settings the enemy grows stronger or counterattacks."

### Task 3: Wire it into the app, and look at it

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/app.ts`
- Test: `tests/baseinfoapp.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `soldierStats`, `buildingInfo` (Task 1); `drawSoldierCard`, `drawInfoBox`, `CARD`, `INFO_BOX` (Task 2); the new hit kinds (Task 2).
- Produces: `App` state `cardName: string | null` and `infoOpen: boolean`.

Behaviour: in the `panel` click handler, `name` selects the row (`panelSelected = index`) and sets `cardName = w.soldiers[index].name`; `info` sets `infoOpen = true`; in the `squad` click handler, `card` sets `cardName = name` without changing `squadPicked`. While `cardName` or `infoOpen` is set, any click on the `panel` or `squad` screen closes it and does nothing else; Escape closes it (and does not leave the screen); the screens' other keys behave as before. `cardName` and `infoOpen` are cleared by `openPanel`, `backToBase`, `backToPlanet`, `confirmSquad` and `toTitle`. The draw code for the `panel` and `squad` screens draws the card (for `cardName`, using the soldier then in `w.soldiers`; if the name no longer exists, nothing and the state is cleared) or the info box (`buildingInfo(this.panelId, w)`) after the screen. A sound click plays on open and close.
